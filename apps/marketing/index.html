How it works

Three moves. Same as on deck.

A jackline is the rope you clip to before crossing the deck. Jackline the product works the same way for AI tools.

01

Clip in

Register your agent (Cursor, Claude Code, internal bots). Mint a connection token — that's your carabiner to Jackline.

02

Explore

Agents call MCP tools through Jackline. Upstreams like Linear, GitHub, and Gmail sit behind one gateway you own.

03

Stay safe

Roles, approvals, and audit logs catch what shouldn't happen. Deny by default — grant only what each builder needs.
